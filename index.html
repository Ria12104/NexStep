Business Model
How NexStep sustains itself while staying student-first
🎓
Free for Students, Always
Core intel browsing, alerts, and verification are permanently free. No paywalls on student knowledge.
🏢
Recruiting Pipeline Listings
Companies pay to post verified recruiting intel, internship timelines, and PPO tips directly to targeted branches and years.
🏛️
Institutional Partnerships
Colleges subscribe to get anonymized trend data — what deadlines students miss, what courses are undervalued, where alumni go.
💎
NexStep Pro (Alumni)
Alumni pay a small fee to maintain their verified contributor status and access cross-college intel after graduation.
📊
Aggregated Insight Reports
EdTech companies and NGOs buy anonymized, aggregated reports on student career outcomes and knowledge gaps by college and branch.
🛡️
Our Commitment
We never sell individual user data. Credibility scores are owned by students. The intel graph stays peer-governed.
